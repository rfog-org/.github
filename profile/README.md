<h2 align="center">RFoG: RF over Glass</h2>

<p align="center">Squad tactics where both sides plan at once.<br>
Free and open source. No ads, no tracking, nothing to buy.</p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://rfog.org"><b>Play at rfog.org</b></a> · <code>ssh -p 2222 yourname@rfog.org</code> · <a href="https://github.com/rfog-org/rfog/releases">Terminal client</a></p>

Two players each lead a commander and four units over a small board with
high ground, cover and fog. Orders are given in secret and the turn resolves for
both sides at once. Ten commanders, chess-style clocks from 10 seconds to a
day a turn, Glicko-2 ratings, challenges by link, live games to watch, and
replays of every match. The browser, the terminal and SSH all play the same
game on the same account.

- **[rfog](https://github.com/rfog-org/rfog)**: the game, its server and
  both clients (Go, AGPL-3.0).
- **Contributing:** bug reports and ideas are welcome in the
  [issues](https://github.com/rfog-org/rfog/issues); the
  [contributing guide](https://github.com/rfog-org/rfog/blob/main/docs/CONTRIBUTING.md)
  covers building, testing and balance.
- **Status:** alpha. Ratings may be reset before the beta.
